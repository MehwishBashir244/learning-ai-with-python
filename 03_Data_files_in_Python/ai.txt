Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that enables machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. These tasks include learning, problem-solving, understanding language, recognizing images, and making decisions.

AI is used in many areas such as healthcare, education, banking, transportation, robotics, and entertainment. Examples include chatbots, voice assistants, recommendation systems, and self-driving technologies. AI can save time, automate repetitive tasks, and help people make better decisions.

In short, AI is a powerful technology that allows computers and machines to learn, think, and perform intelligent tasks.
